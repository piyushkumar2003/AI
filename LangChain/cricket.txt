The morning sun shines on the outfield green,
Where shadows stretch across a tranquil scene.
The toss is called, the heavy coin descends,
And on this pitch, a summer’s fate depends.

The bowler marks his run-up on the grass,
A silent pause before the storm shall pass.
With steady strides, he charges down the track,
And hurls the leather sphere without look back.

The willow strikes, a crisp and ringing sound,
As crimson leather races toward the mound.
The crowd erupts, a sudden thunderous roar,
While signals flash to mark another four.

A sudden twist, the spin bowler arrives,
With deceptive flight, for wickets now he strives.
The batsman steps, but misjudges the seam,
The bails fly high, shattering a dream.

Through long, hot hours beneath the burning sky,
The players fight as endless overs fly.
From patience test to aggressive, quick-fire play,
The game evolves throughout the changing day.

When shadows lengthen and the dusk draws near,
The final run is greeted with a cheer.
Win, draw, or lose, when stumps are pulled at night,
The game remains a noble, shared delight.
